# Code of Conduct

RVISA welcomes respectful technical collaboration across organizations, disciplines, and experience levels. Evaluate proposals on their technical merits, explain disagreements with evidence, and acknowledge the work of others.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Keep project discussions relevant and give contributors a fair opportunity to respond.

Raise conduct concerns privately with an appointed maintainer. Reporting contacts are recorded with maintenance assignments in [MAINTAINERS.yaml](MAINTAINERS.yaml); those assignments are currently open. A maintainer handling a report should protect personal information, consider the affected person's needs, and document a proportionate response.

Technical disagreements are handled through the review process in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
